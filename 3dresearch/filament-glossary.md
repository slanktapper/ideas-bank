## Notes

- **CF and GF are abrasive.** Anything carbon- or glass-filled needs a hardened
  nozzle; brass will wear out quickly.
- **ABS, ASA, PC and nylon want the heated chamber** — the reason the X2D was chosen.
- **Nylons absorb moisture aggressively** and need drying before every use; the AMS 2
  Pro's drying function covers this.
- **Refills** (filament without a spool) are cheaper where offered, and pair with the
  reusable spool.
