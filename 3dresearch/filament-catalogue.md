## The finding that matters

**PETG Basic ($13.74) is cheaper than PLA Basic ($14.29), and ABS ($14.29) costs
exactly the same as PLA.**

The two materials this project actually needs — PETG for functional strength, ABS to
use the 65 °C chamber the printer was chosen for — are the *cheapest* things on the
page. There is no cost argument whatsoever for an all-PLA order.

**ASA at $38.99** is the exception: nearly three times PLA. ASA's advantage over ABS
is UV stability for outdoor parts. Indoors, ABS does the heat-resistance job for a
third of the price. Buy ASA only for parts that will live outside.

## Correction: TPU and the AMS

Earlier research recorded that "the AMS 2 Pro does not support TPU feeding". That was
too absolute. Bambu sells a dedicated **TPU for AMS** (from $44.99) formulated for it.
Standard TPU 95A/85A still is not AMS-compatible; the AMS-specific product is.

## Discount schemes — worth checking before ordering

Two different discounts are visible:

- **Combo Deal** — in the cart, bundled with the printer: PLA Basic at **$16.49**,
  marked down from $29.99.
- **Bulk Sale** — on the collection page: PLA Basic at **$14.29**.

The bulk price is $2.20/spool cheaper. Whether these stack, and whether the bulk sale
has a minimum quantity, is not visible from the collection page. On eight spools the
difference is about $17.60 — worth two minutes at checkout to confirm which applies.
