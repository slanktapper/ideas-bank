# filament.md — what is actually on the shelf

The spools Rob owns, so a project can be designed around filament that exists
rather than filament that would be nice. This is the one place that list lives;
`available-tools.md` points here rather than repeating it.

Three neighbouring files are about something else and are not substitutes:

- `3dresearch/filament-catalogue.md` — Bambu's Canadian **price list**, every
  material they sell. What things cost, not what is here.
- `3dresearch/filament-glossary.md` — what each material **is** and how it
  behaves.
- `3dresearch/materials.md` — which material suits which **job**.

**Keep this current.** It is the file that stops a project quietly assuming a
colour or a material. When spools arrive, move them from *On order* to *On
hand*; when one runs out, take it off. Date every change, Alberta time.

A *refill* is filament with no spool core — it needs an empty Bambu spool to
wind onto. The red, blue, purple, yellow and orange above are refills, so they
top up colours rather than arriving ready to load. Blue and red already exist
on the shelf; purple is close to the indigo purple already there but is not the
same colour.

samples rather than stock.

### Elsewhere, 1.75 mm

| Material | Colour | Brand | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silk PLA | Silk Gold | GIANTARM | 1 kg |
| Silk PLA | Metallic Silver (Silk Silver) | GEEETECH | 1 kg |

Third-party silk PLA is still PLA: same temperatures, same ~60 °C softening,
same brittleness. Silk prints glossy and shows layer lines and seams more than
matte PLA does, so it flatters smooth curved surfaces and punishes flat faces
and sharp corners.

## What is still not here

ASA (UV-stable, outdoor), TPU (flexible), PVA (dissolvable), PC, nylon, and any
carbon-filled composite. Orderable, but with lead time — and the abrasive ones
also need a hardened nozzle that has not been bought. There is no white or pale
PLA beyond jade white, and no clear or transparent filament in any material.

## Colour notes worth keeping

- The only **green** is PLA Basic Mistletoe Green, and it is in the 2026-10-06
  order. Before that there was no green on the shelf at all, so any note older
  than that date which assumes one was assuming wrong.
- **Jade white** and **milky pink** are the two pale PLAs, and they are what a
  small light-coloured part gets. There is no plain white PLA; the white in
  stock is PETG.
- **Eight slots** across two AMS 2 Pro units means eight colours loaded at once
  without swapping. Multi-colour on one part costs purge, so a part split into
  separately printed pieces is usually cheaper than one printed in two colours.
