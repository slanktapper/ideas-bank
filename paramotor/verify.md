Captured 2026-09-07 MDT.

## How this research was gathered, and its limits

This first pass was assembled from web search. The session's network egress proxy
blocked direct access to most manufacturer and dealer sites — including
`flyozone.com`, `dudek.eu`, `apcoaviation.com` and `paramotorplanet.com` — so
**full spec tables could not be read at source.** Figures below came through search
result summaries that quote those pages rather than from the pages themselves.

That is good enough to establish the shape of the problem. It is not good enough to
buy against. Everything in this list needs confirming from the primary source.

## Judgement calls recorded, so they can be re-checked

These are reasoning, not sourced facts, and are where this research is most likely
to be wrong:

- **All-up weight of ~170 kg.** Built up in `sizing.md` from component estimates.
  The motor dry weight (26–32 kg) and fuel load are assumptions. Re-run the
  arithmetic against real spec sheets once candidates are picked.
- **Thrust target of 55–65% of AUW.** A rule of thumb, not a certification. Instructors
  differ on it.
- **Beginner wing loading of 4.0–5.0 kg/m².** Also a rule of thumb. A school may
  reasonably want you lower.
- **"The frame's pilot-weight rating is the binding constraint."** True from the
  published figures found, but only a handful of manufacturers were checked. A
  manufacturer publishing a higher number would change the shortlist significantly.
